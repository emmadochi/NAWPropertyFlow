Executive Overview & Business Value

Real estate development firms in Nigeria suffer up to 15% to 25% capital leakage on site materials through untracked supplier price spikes, ghost deliveries, phantom invoices, and lack of real-time multi-site inventory synchronization. The NAW PropertyFlow Enterprise Suite solves this by interconnecting site engineering with double-entry corporate accounting under strict digital audit controls.

End-to-End Construction Procurement Workflow

How materials move from engineering requisition to supplier delivery and site consumption:

1
Material Requisition (MRF) Submission Site Engineer
The Site Engineer logs needed materials (e.g. 500 bags of Dangote Falcon Cement) for a specific site activity (e.g. 2nd Floor Slab Casting). The system checks against the Quantity Surveyor's Bill of Materials (BOM) template. If requested quantity exceeds allowable variance (+5%), an instant audit flag is raised.
2
Purchase Order (PO) & Market Price Benchmarking Quantity Surveyor / MD
The QS reviews the requisition and generates an official PO. The system automatically benchmarks the requested unit price against current regional market prices (e.g. Dei-Dei Market Abuja or Orile Lagos). Tiered authorization rules ensure POs above ₦5M or ₦20M require Managing Director sign-off.
3
GPS Geofenced Goods Receipt (GRN) & Batch Logging Site Storekeeper
When the supplier's truck arrives at the yard, the Storekeeper inspects the physical goods, records the batch number, manufacturing date (with 90-day cement shelf-life countdown), and captures a live photo. The system confirms the Storekeeper is physically within the geofence perimeter before logging the GRN.
4
Material Issue Voucher (MIV) & Site Wastage Tracking Site Supervisor
Materials are released to site sub-contractors with a digital MIV. If damaged bags or broken blocks exceed standard waste thresholds (1.5x multiplier), a waste log is flagged to site civil management.

Financial Settlement & Double-Entry Accounting Operations

How the finance department verifies invoices and executes audited payouts:

A
Digital Supplier Invoice Submission Supplier Portal
Registered vendors (e.g. Dangote Building Solutions) log into their private Supplier Portal, view approved POs, and upload their tax invoice PDF referencing the PO number.
B
Automated 3-Way Reconciliation Audit Financial Controller
The accounting engine compares:
  • 1. Purchase Order: Approved price & quantity.
  • 2. Goods Received Note: Actual verified site delivery.
  • 3. Supplier Invoice: Billed amount.
If price discrepancy exceeds ±₦500 or quantity billed exceeds delivered quantity, the system blocks payment and alerts the Accountant.
C
Double-Entry General Ledger Posting Automated Ledger
Upon disbursement, the system automatically posts the balanced journal:
[DEBIT] 1300 - Site Materials Inventory (Asset)          ₦4,250,000.00
[CREDIT] 1010 - Corporate Bank Account (Asset)             ₦4,250,000.00
