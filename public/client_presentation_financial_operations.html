Stakeholder Persona Matrix

The platform enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across 6 key roles:

Role / Persona Primary Responsibility Anti-Fraud & Audit Controls
1. Site Engineer Submits Material Requisitions (MRF) for specific building milestones. Cannot exceed QS Bill of Materials (BOM) without variance approval.
2. Quantity Surveyor (QS) Sets material ratios, checks market price benchmarks, and creates POs. Audited against regional price benchmarks (Abuja Dei-Dei / Lagos Orile).
3. Managing Director (MD) Authorizes high-value Purchase Orders and corporate disbursements. Tiered authorization: 1-click approval for POs exceeding ₦5M / ₦20M.
4. Site Storekeeper Inspects delivered goods, logs batches, and issues materials on site. GPS Geofence locked (300m radius) + 90-day cement shelf-life tracker.
5. Financial Controller Audits 3-way matching, approves payouts, and monitors balance sheets. Zero manual data entry; automated balanced journal generation.
6. Verified Supplier Uploads digital PDF invoices and tracks payment status. Access limited strictly to private Supplier Portal with PO matching.

End-to-End Site Procurement Flow (Step-by-Step)

1
Step 1: Bill of Materials (BOM) Standard Definition Quantity Surveyor
The Quantity Surveyor establishes the engineering standard for each structural activity before building begins.
Example (Grade 25 Concrete Slab - 1 m³):
• 7.0 Bags Dangote Falcon Cement (50kg) @ ₦8,500/bag
• 0.5 Trips Coarse River Sand @ ₦120,000/trip
• 0.8 Trips 3/4 Clean Granite Stone @ ₦280,000/trip
• Allowable Site Tolerance: ±5.00%
2
Step 2: Material Requisition Form (MRF) Submission Site Engineer
When work begins on the 1st Floor Suspended Slab Casting at Hutu Prestige Estate, the Site Engineer logs into the mobile portal and requests 500 bags of cement.
  • The system verifies that 500 bags match the remaining casting volume ($71.4\text{ m}^3 \times 7\text{ bags}$).
  • If the engineer enters `700 bags`, the system blocks submission with an Over-Allocation Red Alert (+40%) requiring civil justification.
3
Step 3: Purchase Order (PO) & Tiered Authorization QS & Managing Director
The QS converts the approved MRF into an official Purchase Order (PO-2026-0042) assigned to Dangote Building Solutions Ltd.
  • Price Benchmark Check: Quoted ₦8,500/bag is compared with Dei-Dei Market index (₦8,500). Variance = 0%.
  • Total Value: $500 \times ₦8,500 = \mathbf{₦4,250,000.00}$.
  • Approval Tier: Under ₦5,000,000 threshold $\rightarrow$ Authorized instantly by QS/Project Manager.
4
Step 4: GPS Geofenced Goods Receipt (GRN) on Site Site Storekeeper
The supplier's flatbed truck arrives at Katampe Extension Site Yard.
  • Geofence Verification: The Storekeeper's mobile browser confirms GPS presence within 300m of Plot 402 Katampe Extension ($9.0820^\circ\text{ N}, 7.4810^\circ\text{ E}$).
  • Batch & Quality Inspection: Physical tally (500 bags), Batch No: DAN-ABJ-882, Live photo of stacked pallets uploaded.
  • Shelf-Life Countdown: System initiates a 90-day timer for cement freshness.
5
Step 5: Material Issuance (MIV) & Wastage Tracking Storekeeper & Sub-contractor
When site masons withdraw 200 bags for today's casting, the Storekeeper generates a digital Material Issue Voucher (MIV).
  • Site stock automatically reduces from 500 bags to 300 bags in real time.
  • If damaged bags exceed standard site tolerance (1.5x multiplier), an automated **Wastage Alert** is routed to Project Directors.
