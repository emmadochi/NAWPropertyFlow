Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA 2023) Alignment

How NAW PropertyFlow protects property developers and client personal data under Nigerian law.

1. Data Controller vs. Data Processor Roles
Statutory Clarity

Under the NDPA 2023, the Developer is the Data Controller retaining complete ownership and lawful purpose over buyer data. NAW PropertyFlow acts strictly as the Data Processor, processing information exclusively under written instructions without third-party monetization.

2. Client KYC & Sensitive Data Protection
KYC Vault

Buyer identification documents (NIN, Passports, utility bills) uploaded to the Diaspora Portal or Legal Document Factory are encrypted and stored in private vaults accessible only by authorized compliance officers.

3. Data Subject Rights & Portability
Zero Vendor Lock-in

In full compliance with NDPA Section 34, developers can export 100% of their raw plot inventory, transaction histories, client records, and legal archives in standardized formats (CSV, JSON, SQL) anytime.

Infrastructure Security

Cryptographic Architecture & Access Governance

Multi-layered defensive architecture protecting financial records and plot allocation ledgers.

1. Multi-Tenant Database Isolation
Logical Separation

Each developer instance operates with dedicated tenant identification scoping. Database queries are strictly partitioned at the application layer, guaranteeing that no developer or broker can view records from another organization.

2. Encryption Standards (At-Rest & In-Transit)
AES-256 / TLS 1.3

All network traffic between client browsers, diaspora portals, and our servers is secured via TLS 1.3. Database tables, files, and transaction logs are encrypted at rest using AES-256 cryptographic algorithms.

3. Immutable Action Audit Logs
Full Forensics

Every critical operational event (plot allocation, receipt generation, commission approval, permission changes) is recorded in an immutable audit ledger with timestamps, user IP addresses, and before-and-after change diffs.

Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

Enforce strict departmental separation (e.g. Sales reps cannot view company payroll; Front Desk cannot approve commissions; Realtors cannot view total land acquisition costs).
