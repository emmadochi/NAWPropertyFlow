The Five Operational Leaks Draining Developer Margins

Real estate development companies rarely fail due to lack of land or buyer demand. They fail due to operational opacity—when spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats, and disconnected accounting cannot scale with transaction volume.

1. "Who owns this plot?"
The Leak: Disconnected spreadsheets cause double-reservations, lost reservation fees, and conflicting plot allocations.
NAW Solution: Centralized real-time inventory engine with automatic plot locking during active reservations.
2. "Who has paid what?"
The Leak: Buyers on 6–24 month payment plans miss installments while accounts teams waste hours compiling manual ledgers.
NAW Solution: Automated milestone payment engine with SMS/email due reminders and 1-click Statements of Account (SOA).
3. "Who gets how much commission?"
The Leak: Unclear realtor splits and delayed payouts cause disputes, driving top external brokers to rival developers.
NAW Solution: Automated commission ledger with multi-tier team splits, override rules, and instant payout vouchers.
4. "Why aren't our leads closing?"
The Leak: 60% of high-intent inquiries drop off after site inspections because sales reps fail to execute structured follow-ups.
NAW Solution: Automated inspection scheduling, driver/agent dispatch, and systematic WhatsApp/email nurturing flows.
5. "Can our diaspora buyer see everything from anywhere in the world?"
The Leak: High-net-worth diaspora buyers in the UK, US, and Canada hesitate or pause installments due to fear of fraud and lack of verified construction reporting.
NAW Solution: Dedicated 24/7 Diaspora Investor Portal providing live payment ledgers, downloadable official PDF receipts, and verified milestone drone/photo progress.
