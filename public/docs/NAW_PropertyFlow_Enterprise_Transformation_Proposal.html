Current Operational Architecture & Risk Points

Based on our consultative operational audit, here is an executive synthesis of existing workflow bottlenecks and their business impact.

1. Dispersed Spreadsheet Plot Registers
High Operational Risk

Observed State: Plot allocations across active estates reside in separate Excel workbooks managed by multiple staff. Real-time availability is uncertain during high-volume sales launches.
Target State: Unified cloud inventory register with automated plot locking during reservations and instant executive layout occupancy analytics.

2. Delayed Installment Follow-Up & SOA Reconciliation
Cash Flow Leakage

Observed State: Accounts team manually reviews bank statements and compiles customer statements in Microsoft Word/Excel upon request, delaying collection velocity.
Target State: Automated installment milestone schedules with 7-day proactive SMS/email due reminders and 1-click audit-ready PDF Statements of Account.

3. Realtor Split Ambiguity & Manual Commission Vouchers
Realtor Churn Risk

Observed State: Commission split calculations between agency heads and sub-agents are processed manually via WhatsApp, leading to occasional disputes.
Target State: Automated commission ledger with multi-tier percentage rules, team overrides, and instant digital payout vouchers.

Strategic Outcome:

Transitioning these manual workflows to NAW PropertyFlow eliminates operational friction, accelerates cash collections, and elevates your corporate brand with diaspora buyers.
