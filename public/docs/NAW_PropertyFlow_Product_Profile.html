1. Smart Plot & Layout Allocation Engine

Centralized inventory control preventing double-allocations and managing land prototype layouts.

Multi-Estate Plot, Unit & Prototype Registry
Why It Matters: Double-allocating a single plot in high-value areas like Guzape or Katampe destroys developer reputation and triggers severe legal liabilities. NAW PropertyFlow eliminates this risk completely.

Tracks every plot number, square meterage (e.g., 250sqm, 500sqm, 1,000sqm), prototype building type (Fully Detached Duplex, Terrace, Penthouse), layout coordinates, and live status (Available, Reserved, Under Inspection, Allocated, Sold).

Instant Real-time Estate Occupancy Rate
Plot Locking during inspection/booking
Phase & Sector layout categorization
Plot swapping & reassignment history
Cash Flow Security

2. Milestone Installment & Billing Engine

Automated payment plans, scheduled milestone reminders, and instant digital statements of account.

Automated Nigerian Payment Schedules & SOA
Why It Matters: Un-tracked installment payments freeze project cash flow. Automated milestone alerts ensure 95%+ on-time debt collection without uncomfortable manual confrontations.

Configures flexible installment structures (Outright, 3-Month, 6-Month, 12-Month, 24-Month). Automatically computes principal paid, outstanding balances, overdue penalties, and issues instant Statements of Account (SOA).

Automated SMS & Email due-date alerts
1-Click PDF Payment Receipts & Invoices
Direct Bank Transfer reconciliation
Comprehensive Client Ledger audit trail
